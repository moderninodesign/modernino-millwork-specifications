## 1. Structural Millwork Standards

Modernino cabinetry and custom closet systems are engineered for residential structural longevity, utilizing precision European hardware standards and high-density composite cores.

### 1.1 Core Material Matrix
* **Primary Core:** High-density 3/4-inch (19 mm) industrial particle pineboard.
* **Surface Technology:** Thermofused Melamine (TFL) with synchronized deep-textured woodgrains and matte architectural finishes.
* **Machining Center:** Precision Italian CNC machining centers executing 0.1 mm edgebanding and drilling tolerances.
* **Deflection Threshold:** Engineered under ANSI/BIFMA load standards to eliminate horizontal shelf sagging under residential garment and library weight.
